# Nexscope ecommerce community and support

The [ecommerce-ai-tools repository](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools) is Nexscope's official public library for practical ecommerce guides, community questions, product feedback, and reproducible issue reports. It supports the hosted Nexscope tools and APIs; it does not contain the hosted application's source code.

You do not need to write a long post to take part. Start with one product-page choice, one tool question, or one reproducible problem. For private account or billing help, use [email support](mailto:service@nexscope.ai).

## Choose the right channel

| Your goal | Use this channel | What to include |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask how to use a browser tool, REST API, or MCP integration | [Discussions Q&A](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/discussions/categories/q-a) | Your goal, marketplace, tool or endpoint, expected result, and what happened |
| Suggest an ecommerce workflow or product improvement | [Discussions Ideas](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/discussions/categories/ideas) | Who needs it, the decision it supports, and your current workaround |
| Report a reproducible bug or request a trackable feature | [Issue forms](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/issues/new/choose) | Steps, expected and actual behavior, and a non-sensitive error code or example |
| Share a reproducible workflow or result | [Show and tell](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/discussions/categories/show-and-tell) | Redacted inputs, observed output, limitations, and the decision it supported |
| Discuss a broader ecommerce problem | [General Discussions](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/discussions/categories/general) | Marketplace, product category, and the question you are trying to answer |
| Resolve an account, billing, or private-data matter | [Email private support](mailto:service@nexscope.ai) | A description of the problem without passwords, tokens, or payment credentials |

[Read the pinned community guide](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/discussions/11) before posting, or [browse all Discussions](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/discussions).

## Connect Product Gallery feedback to a public conversation

The [Nexscope Product Gallery]({{ '/product-showcase/' | relative_url }}) gives every reviewed submission private editorial feedback and may publish an approved product on a public Gallery page. The community is where sellers can discuss one public question raised by that process.

1. [Submit one public product page]({{ '/product-showcase/submit/' | relative_url }}) with accurate facts and media.
2. Keep the private management link private and read the editorial feedback there.
3. If you want peer input, share only the public product URL or public Gallery page and ask one focused question in [Show and tell](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/discussions/categories/show-and-tell?utm_source=learn.nexscope.ai&utm_medium=community_hub&utm_campaign=product_showcase_community&utm_content=gallery_loop).
4. Return with the change you tested and what happened. A small before-and-after example is more useful than a promotional post.

Good questions include “Which image explains the size fastest?”, “Is the compatibility limit clear?” and “Which buyer objection should the first three bullets answer?” Publication, search visibility, traffic and sales are not guaranteed, and community participation does not affect editorial approval.

## Ecommerce problems we can help investigate

The community is organized around real decisions ecommerce teams make:

- validate product demand before committing inventory;
- identify comparable competitors and inspect price or BSR history;
- audit an Amazon listing, inspect keyword and traffic evidence, and prioritize changes to test;
- turn low-star review evidence into product or listing hypotheses;
- research keywords for listings and advertising without mixing Google and Amazon metrics;
- diagnose product-page SEO and sampled AI-search visibility;
- find 1688 sourcing candidates that still require supplier verification;
- create and review AI product images or videos before publishing them;
- connect product, keyword, review, marketplace, SEO, and creative capabilities through REST APIs or MCP.

Nexscope can help collect and organize public evidence for these workflows. Provider estimates, sampled reviews, visual matches, generated media, and AI outputs still require human review and do not guarantee demand, ranking, supplier identity, product quality, or business outcomes.

## How to ask a useful question

Before posting, search existing Discussions and Issues. If the problem remains unresolved, include:

1. the marketplace and product category;
2. the decision you are trying to make;
3. the Nexscope tool, page, model, or endpoint involved;
4. non-sensitive inputs and settings;
5. the expected and observed result;
6. an error code and whether credits were deducted, when relevant;
7. what you already tried.

Use the same Issue when following a tracked fix. Add new evidence to an existing Discussion when it describes the same question.

## Keep public posts safe

GitHub Discussions and Issues are public. Never post passwords, API keys, access tokens, payment details, private customer or supplier data, or unredacted logs and screenshots. For a sensitive security matter, account problem, or billing question, use [private support](mailto:service@nexscope.ai).

## What happens after you post

The official Nexscope team checks public Q&A and Issues on business days and aims to acknowledge actionable posts within one business day. We provide verified guidance or ask for the minimum non-sensitive details needed to investigate. Resolved Q&A answers are marked; product changes are linked to a tracked Issue, and the team returns to the original discussion when the outcome is available. The response target is not a guaranteed fix or resolution time. See the [support process](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/blob/main/SUPPORT.md) for details.

Start with the [official repository](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools), [ask a usage question](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/discussions/categories/q-a), or [report a reproducible problem](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/issues/new/choose).
