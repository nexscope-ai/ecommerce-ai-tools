# Businesses in ChatGPT: how to claim your business and read your insights

OpenAI launched **Businesses in ChatGPT** in beta on November 10, 2026. Eligible businesses can claim their profile in **Business Hub** and view aggregate mentions and website clicks after verification. If your next decision is what to fix on one product page, review how AI answers identify the exact product and support any recommendation. [OpenAI launch announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)

## How do you claim your business in ChatGPT?

Start with the official claim page and sign in. You must own the business or be authorized to represent it. Claim availability depends on the business type, location and supported verification methods. Shopify is one verification route. An Ads account or Enterprise agreement is optional. [OpenAI claim page](https://openai.com/business/businesses-in-chatgpt/) · [OpenAI setup instructions](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

1. Open [ChatGPT Business Hub](https://chatgpt.com/business-hub) and search for your business by name. Select the matching listing. If you run an eligible online business and cannot find it, look for the option to add it.
2. Complete a verification method offered for your listing. Depending on the business, options can include a business phone, business email, website domain or Shopify.
3. Complete identity verification through Persona. After approval, open your profile to update information, connect an eligible product feed or view available insights.

Retailers can use a feed to supply current product information such as price and availability. Keep those fields consistent with the landing page. OpenAI documents separate eligibility for **Business Agents**, initially available to eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses with a website. Publishing an agent is optional. [OpenAI setup instructions](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt) · [Business Agents policy](https://openai.com/policies/business-agents-policy/)

## What do ChatGPT mentions and website clicks measure?

Business Hub summarizes available activity over the **past 28 days**. For an online business, a mention is counted when ChatGPT references the business’s website. Seeing the brand name by itself in an answer can therefore differ from seeing a recorded mention. Local listings follow a different definition. [OpenAI's Insights definitions](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt#understand-your-profile-insights)

| Business Hub insight | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Mentions | Record whether references to the business website change over comparable reporting periods. |
| Website clicks | Check how often users click through, then use your own analytics to see which pages receive visits and what visitors do there. |
| Discovery topics | Turn a recurring theme into a specific buying question you can investigate for one product. |

Mark a blank metric as **unavailable**. Reporting thresholds and data collection can leave a field blank. Discovery topics summarize themes across answers, and OpenAI keeps individual prompts and chats private. [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

## Turn a discovery topic into a product-page decision

Suppose a discovery topic points to painting tools for high walls. Pick one roller kit and one target market. Test a buying question you can act on, such as “Which paint roller kit has an extension pole suitable for painting a stairwell?”

1. **Read the answer.** Save the question, date, market and any cited link. Record whether the answer names the brand, identifies the exact kit or explicitly recommends it for the job.
2. **Check the claim against the listing.** If a buyer needs to know the working length with the roller attached, look for that measurement and the assembly details on the product page.
3. **Verify one change.** If the listing leaves the measurement unclear, measure the assembled kit and update the relevant bullet or image. Save the revised page and repeat the same question later with comparable settings.
