# TikTok Shop Product and Creator APIs: a research workflow

Finding a TikTok product with sales is only the first step. A useful creator shortlist also needs market, product fit, promotion evidence, and a reason the creator is relevant to the audience.

Nexscope documents TikTok Shop product search, product videos, creator search, creator analytics, video search, live research, and related-product capabilities. These can be combined into a repeatable affiliate research workflow.

**Quick answer:** Start with a product or category, record the market and date, connect products to related videos and creators, and rank candidates by audience and product fit. Treat sales and promotion fields as documented signals, not guarantees of future performance.

## A practical creator shortlist

Start with a product or category, then collect:

- supported market;
- product and shop details;
- related videos or lives;
- creator profile and audience signals;
- available sales or promotion indicators;
- the content angle used in the video;
- date and source of every observation.

Rank candidates by fit and evidence, not by follower count alone.

## Turn content into a brief

For each promising creator or video, document:

1. the first three seconds of the hook;
2. the customer problem shown;
3. the product proof used;
4. the offer or call to action;
5. comments that reveal objections;
6. the angle your brand could test without copying the original.

This turns research into a production brief while preserving the difference between observed content and your proposed idea.
