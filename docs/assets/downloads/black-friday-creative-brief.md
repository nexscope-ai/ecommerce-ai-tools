## Four-asset exercise

1. Clean product still: unchanged item on a simple background.
2. Gift-context still: same item in one appropriate setting, with no implied extra accessories.
3. Motion clip: one simple camera direction with the item preserved.
4. Presenter concept: one verified feature explained without a fictional testimonial.

## Fictional organizer brief

Buyer question: Where can I keep the small things on my desk?

Supported line, only if the selected item actually has the compartments: These compartments keep everyday desk items separated.

Prompt direction: Preserve the selected organizer's shape, color, compartments and quantity. Add no dimensions, discount, delivery guarantee, accessory or product-performance claim.

Presenter next step: Check the size and included pieces on the product page, then see whether it fits your setup.
