# Editorial and evidence policy

**Nexscope publishes practical ecommerce guidance with the source, date and limitation kept close to the claim.** A tool result can support a follow-up question or test; it does not automatically prove demand, causality, future performance or business impact.

## Evidence labels

| Label | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Observed result | A stated input produced a recorded output at a stated time. |
| Official documentation | A capability or policy is described by the organization responsible for it. |
| Provider estimate | A returned metric is directional unless its methodology and accuracy are independently established. |
| Proposed workflow | A practical next step has been designed but its outcome has not yet been measured. |
| Unknown | The available evidence cannot support a value or conclusion. |

## Source hierarchy

For changing products, APIs, policies and search features, Nexscope prefers primary sources: official documentation, announcements, policies and public product pages. Secondary analysis may provide context, but it does not replace the primary source for a current capability or rule.

For Nexscope tools, the live API documentation and product interface are the source of truth for current fields, access, supported markets and credit requirements.

## Dated tests and examples

An API evidence record should identify the environment, test date, input, requested scope, observed return and known limitations. Examples and screenshots are labeled when they are illustrative rather than measured.

One successful request does not guarantee identical results for every input. One failed request does not establish that a capability is universally unavailable.

## AI-assisted analysis

AI may help organize or draft material, but claims that depend on returned evidence are checked against the available source. Unsupported interpretations are removed or labeled as hypotheses. AI-generated images, videos or speakers are not presented as real customer experiences.

## Comparisons and commercial relationships

Comparisons focus on workflow fit, documented capabilities and representative tests. Similar field names do not prove equivalent sources, coverage, freshness or methodology.

Links to Nexscope may include campaign parameters for attribution. They do not change the editorial conclusion. When a page discusses another vendor, its current official documentation should be checked before making a purchase or implementation decision.

## Updates, corrections and review dates

Pages display a last-reviewed date when the underlying capability or guidance has been checked. Material corrections should update the review date and preserve the limitation needed to interpret the result.

To report a reproducible issue or correction, use the [Nexscope GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/issues/new/choose). For general questions, see [community and support]({{ '/community/' | relative_url }}).
