Free self-assessment

Ecommerce site audit prioritizer

This free ecommerce audit tool asks nine checks about your store. It creates a readiness snapshot and puts the highest-risk work first. It runs in your browser and does not crawl, save, or send your site data.

Can search engines reach and index your important category and product URLs?

Consider robots rules, noindex directives, response codes, rendering, and URL Inspection examples.

Do canonical, robots, redirects, and sitemap signals agree?

Compare the URLs you want indexed with the canonical and index signals Google actually sees.

Can a crawler reach priority products through normal category links?

Check category depth, pagination, descriptive anchors, and whether key products are hidden behind interactions.

Does each category and product template answer a distinct shopper question?

Look for useful selection guidance, accurate product details, and content that is not copied across templates.

Do visible price, availability, variants, and structured data agree?

Compare the page, Product or Offer markup, and Merchant Center feed where those surfaces are used.

Have you decided which filters deserve indexable landing pages?

Separate useful search destinations from color, size, sort, or other parameter combinations that create crawl waste.

Have you checked mobile UX and separated lab findings from field data?

Review representative templates on mobile; do not treat one Lighthouse run as a history of real-user performance.

Do Search Console and analytics baselines exist for the pages you will change?

Record impressions, clicks, CTR, conversions, date range, market, device, and the exact URLs before editing.

Does important buying content lead with an evidence-backed answer?

Check whether the page states its method, date, limits, recommendation, and next action instead of only repeating keywords.
