# How to create an AI avatar product video

**Create an AI avatar product video by adding your product images, selecting a presenter and giving Nexscope accurate product facts or selling points.** Use the AI writing assistant to explore script plans, edit the chosen direction, then select available video settings and review the generated clip before using it.

{% include article-action.html placement="article-top" %}

> **Quick answer:** Avatar Video combines product material with a selected AI presenter and a written direction. Start with one SKU, one presenter and one message. Review AI script suggestions before generation, then check product identity, speech and interactions in the complete output. A presenter-led ad is not evidence of a real customer's experience.

## When should you use an avatar product video?

**Use an avatar when a person explaining the product would help communicate one clear idea.** A short introduction, feature explanation or scene-led product story can benefit from a presenter. A simple moving packshot may be easier to make with [Image to Video]({{ '/ai-video-generator/' | relative_url }}).

The [Nexscope AI UGC page](https://www.nexscope.ai/ai-ugc-video-generator) shows product, talking-video and reference-led creative examples. This tutorial focuses on the **Avatar Video** workspace. For the wider process of turning research into a creative brief, read the existing [TikTok Shop AI UGC workflow]({{ '/ecommerce-trends/tiktok-shop-ai-ugc-video-seller-workflow/' | relative_url }}).

Choose a message that can be supported by the product material you provide. If the message depends on a measured result, such as load capacity or battery life, a generated scene cannot supply that evidence.

## What product material should you prepare?

**Gather clear images of one exact product, a few approved facts and a short description of the intended audience.** Keep packaging, color and included items consistent across the images.

For an illustrative desk-organizer brief, prepare:

- A front image and a side image of the same organizer.
- A clear view of its compartments and finish.
- Approved wording about the features actually shown.
- One scene: a presenter introducing it on a desk.
- One next action: view the organizer's product details.

This is a writing exercise, not a tested product or generation result. Replace every detail with facts about your own item. Do not add dimensions, material claims or compatibility merely because they would make the script more persuasive.

Open **Avatar Video**, sign in and add the product images. The allowed image count and available model settings are shown in the workspace. A product URL can provide useful context, but it does not replace the required product images in this workflow. If you want to begin by importing a listing, the [URL-to-video guide]({{ '/url-to-video-guide/' | relative_url }}) covers that separate route.

## How do you choose the AI presenter?

**Choose an available avatar that fits the scene and keeps attention on the product.** Start with one presenter so the first review is easier to interpret.

The current web interface offers an avatar library and a custom-avatar image upload. It allows up to two selected avatars; the live interface governs what is available to your account. For a custom image, use a person or character you have permission to use and check the accepted file requirements shown by the upload control.

Consider framing and product interaction before choosing appearance. A desk introduction may need only a presenter beside the organizer. Asking for a complex hand demonstration can create more opportunities for the item or fingers to change between frames.

Selecting or uploading an avatar image does not by itself establish voice cloning, identity replication or an endorsement. Describe the intended presentation rather than inventing a personal history for the presenter.

## How do you create and edit the script direction?

**Enter accurate selling points and use the AI writing assistant to review alternative script plans.** Apply the plan that best fits your goal, then edit the resulting prompt before generation.

The current implementation requests multiple script proposals from the supplied product images and context. Usable proposals depend on the response; do not assume every request produces a fixed number of successful choices. You can review a plan, refresh supported options and apply a selected plan to the prompt field.

Use this illustrative brief as a starting point:

```text
Create a short presenter-led introduction for the desk organizer
shown in the supplied images. Use one presenter beside the product.
Keep its shape, finish and visible compartments consistent.
Open with a question about keeping small desk items together.
Show the organizer, then invite viewers to check the product details.
Use clear English. Do not invent dimensions, a discount, a personal
review or a claim that the presenter has used the product.
```

If you want spoken wording, add a short preferred line, such as: “Looking for a place to keep your everyday desk items? Take a closer look at this organizer.” Treat it as requested wording and check the generated audio; this workflow does not guarantee a verbatim reading.

Cut anything that conflicts with the images or approved product facts. Recheck the prompt after changing the product, market or language. A script plan prepared for earlier inputs may no longer fit the revised brief.

## Which video settings should you check?

**Review the available model, duration, resolution, aspect ratio, market and language as one set.** Supported options vary by model and workflow, so choose from the current controls rather than copying a configuration from a different model review.

| Setting | Decision to make |
| --- | --- |
| Aspect ratio | Which frame fits the intended placement? |
| Duration | Can the introduction fit without rushing the message? |
| Language and market | Does the wording suit the intended audience? |
| Resolution and model | Are the current options suitable for the product detail? |
| Credit estimate | Is this the configuration you intend to pay for? |

The [pricing page](https://www.nexscope.ai/pricing) provides current plan information. Trial credits do not mean every model or configuration is free or available. Review the estimate and account access before submitting once, then follow the task status.

## What should you inspect in the finished video?

**Watch the complete clip with sound, then review it again at the intended display size.** The quality of the presenter and the accuracy of the product are separate checks.

| Check | Keep the clip when… | Revise it when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product | Shape, color and visible details match | Compartments, packaging or accessories change |
| Interaction | Gestures fit the scene and item | Hands pass through or reshape the product |
| Speech | The message matches approved facts | Audio adds an offer, promise or testimonial |
| Presentation | The product remains easy to see | The presenter blocks the feature being described |
| Text | Any visible labels or captions are accurate | Words become unreadable or unsupported |

The pre-existing [Nexscope creator-led sandal demonstration](https://www.nexscope.ai/video-lab/ugc-sandal-review.mp4) illustrates the presenter-led format. It is an official creative example, not a customer review or an output from the desk-organizer brief above.

If the result fails, change one likely cause before generating again: simplify the action, improve the source image or shorten the script. Keep the prompt, selected inputs, settings and review decision so you can compare versions. Check the destination's current disclosure and posting requirements before publishing.

## Common questions about AI avatar product videos

### Can I write the direction myself?

Yes. Provide your own product direction and edit the prompt directly. AI script plans are optional assistance; your supplied facts should remain the basis of the message.

### Can I use two presenters?

The current web interface allows up to two selected avatars. Start with one for a simpler first test, and check the available settings and prompt limits when changing the selection.

### Can the presenter give a customer testimonial?

A generated presenter does not establish a real customer's purchase or experience. Keep the message factual and avoid invented first-person reviews.
