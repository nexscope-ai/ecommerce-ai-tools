# AI Visibility Checker for Products: Test Three Buyer Questions

Use an AI visibility checker to see whether an AI answer names, describes or recommends your exact product. A quick way to start is to run three buyer questions and record the evidence. That gives you a baseline you can compare after improving the product page.

## Use three questions with different purposes

Choose one public product URL and one target market. Write down the brand, model and variant before testing. Run each question in a fresh conversation and record the date, platform and market settings that are visible.

| Question type | Example for a 1 L stainless-steel bottle | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unbranded discovery | “Which 1 L stainless-steel bottles fit a standard bike bottle cage?” | Whether the exact product appears and meets the stated need |
| Branded verification | “Does the River Bottle 1 L fit a standard bike bottle cage?” | Whether the answer names the right model and supports the fit claim |
| Purchase comparison | “What should I check before choosing River Bottle 1 L or Summit Bottle 1 L for cycling?” | Whether the comparison uses verified dimensions and comparable models |

Use your real product details in place of the illustrative names. Keep screenshots or response links when available, and record the page cited next to the exact claim it supports.

## Log the result at the claim level

If an answer names the brand but lists another model, mark it as a brand mention. If it describes the right product but omits a crucial limitation, note the missing fact. If it explicitly suggests the product for the stated use, record the recommendation and verify the reasoning against the product page. Save each result separately so that a branded answer cannot make an unbranded discovery test look successful.

When a result is incorrect, identify the smallest fact you can verify and publish. For the bottle, a measured outer diameter can be more useful than “fits most holders.” Repeat the same question after a substantive page change and keep the original result for comparison. Output can vary between runs, so record differences as observations.

## When to request a deeper review

If a product is repeatedly confused with another variant or competitors appear for your buyer questions, send one product URL, the target market and the sample questions that exposed the gap.
