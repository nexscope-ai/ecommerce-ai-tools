# Product ad images: write a brief that produces a reviewable creative pack

**A product advertising image brief should explain what each image must communicate and which product details must remain accurate.** Nexscope's advertising image service lets you request a chosen quantity, overall direction and individual image requirements, with review before delivery. Start with a small pack for a specific placement instead of an open-ended request to make the product look better.

## Give each image a different job

Download the [product advertising image brief]({{ '/assets/downloads/product-ad-image-brief.md' | relative_url }}). Record the exact item, approved references, target audience, placement, required visual information and claims to exclude.

| Creative job | Brief direction | Product check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the offer | Product-first composition with clear contents | Included pieces and quantity match |
| Explain the context | A relevant setting around the item | Props are not presented as included equipment |
| Clarify a detail | Close view of a real component | Geometry, connection and proportion match the reference |

These are three suggested jobs, not a fixed service bundle. The current form supports 1–20 images, priced per image. Select what you need and inspect the total quote before confirming.

## Write the brief in four parts

**Product:** identify the exact variant, visible parts and what is included. Provide a clear reference and extra detail views where the brief needs information the main view cannot show.

**Audience and setting:** say what the image is for and what context helps the buyer. Distinguish a scene prop from an item included in the offer.

**Composition and size:** choose Auto sizing or specify custom dimensions for a defined destination. Auto selects a suitable size for the direction; it does not promise unlimited resolution. Check the actual pixels and crop of delivered files against the destination requirements.

**Text and evidence:** state whether you want a text-free concept or approved wording. Do not use the image to imply certifications, strength, reach or accessories that have not been established.

## Approve the product before judging the scene

Compare each delivered original with the real reference. Check quantity, component shape, labels, connectors and the relationship between props and included contents. Confirm visible wording against approved product facts. A strong composition can still depict the wrong item.

Keep the image ID, actual dimensions, reviewer and decision. A delivery review does not remove the merchant's need to inspect the chosen asset for its specific placement. Test campaign performance only after a factual and destination check; creative approval alone does not predict advertising results.
