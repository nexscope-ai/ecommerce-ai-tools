# TikTok Shop GMV Max creative checklist for Black Friday

Pick one hero SKU and make sure each eligible video answers a different shopper question about it. TikTok's US BFCM seller guide lists a November 11–30 campaign window in Pacific time. TikTok's Product GMV Max documentation describes creative sources such as connected accounts and authorized affiliate videos. Check the actual campaign and asset eligibility in your account. [TikTok Shop BFCM seller guide](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?content_id=2786070166210347) · [TikTok creative asset eligibility](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-make-creative-assets-available-for-your-product-gmv-max-campaign?lang=en-39)

## Brief three videos for the same product

For a travel mug, pick one color and pack configuration. Give every creator the exact product link, approved facts, what ships and the current promotion conditions. The example below gives each video a different job while keeping the SKU constant.

| Concept | First shot | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product demo | Show the actual mug, then remove and replace the lid | The lid and parts match the linked listing |
| Fit check | Show a ruler across the base or place it in a measured holder | The claim gives the measurement and avoids implying universal fit |
| What's included | Lay out everything that comes in one order | The linked variant matches the quantity, color and accessories on camera |

## Review product links and permissions before launch

Open the shoppable post and confirm it points to the exact SKU. Check that the post is available to the campaign and that creator authorization and required disclosures are in place. If a clip is missing, review permissions and its product link before assuming the footage itself needs to change. Verify the current checkout offer and delivery details before putting them in the creative. [TikTok Product GMV Max guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-product-gmv-max)

When you review results, save the asset ID, SKU, reporting period, spend and product clicks where available. TikTok says Product GMV Max reporting can include organic and affiliate orders for promoted products. Use the metric definitions in your account before interpreting a reported order or GMV change. [TikTok Product GMV Max attribution](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-product-gmv-max)

## Give the creator one verified product brief

If the listing and videos disagree on what ships, first fix the product facts. If you also have a public Amazon or independent-store product page for that SKU, use it to request **Product copy rewriting** or **Product advertising images** in the Nexscope Product Growth Report. Share approved copy and images with the creator as references for the video. [Product Growth Report scope](https://learn.nexscope.ai/product-growth-report/)
