# Amazon Shop Direct for DTC brands: a one-product catalog checklist

Amazon introduced the Shop Direct Merchant Workspace on November 4, 2026. Eligible independent merchants can connect their catalogs so selected products may appear on Amazon and in Alexa for Shopping. Start by checking one product and its exact variant against the public store page. [Amazon's Merchant Workspace announcement](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-shop-direct-merchant-workspace)

## Set up the store and choose a catalog route

Use Amazon's Merchant Workspace to create a merchant account and verify your business website. Amazon describes catalog sharing through supported providers and a direct Selling Partner API route. Pick the route your account supports and use its current instructions for field requirements. [Amazon's setup and catalog options](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-shop-direct-merchant-workspace)

Shop Direct can send a shopper to your store to complete the purchase. Amazon also describes Buy for Me for eligible products. Your team still handles stock, pricing, fulfillment, returns and customer service. [Amazon's description of the shopping experience](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-shop-direct-external-stores)

## Check one SKU before sharing the catalog

Open the URL from the catalog in a fresh browser session and select the US market. Compare it with the expected offer.

| Check | Catalog and page should agree on |
| --- | --- |
| Item identity | Brand, model, variant and pack quantity |
| Landing page | The exact variant a shopper can select |
| Offer | Current price, currency and stock for that variant |
| What ships | Included items and any accessory sold separately |
| Delivery and returns | The terms a shopper sees on your store |

For example, if the catalog row describes a two-pack at $32 but the URL opens a single mug at $19, correct the variant mapping before sharing the row. If the link is correct and the title leaves pack size unclear, update the page where a shopper can see it. Keep a dated record of what you changed.

## Review the submitted product and improve its landing page

Check the active status shown in the Workspace after sharing your catalog. When you observe a Shop Direct result, open the linked page and compare the variant and offer again. Treat visibility as an observation for the query and session you used.
