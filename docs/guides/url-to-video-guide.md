# How to make a product video from a listing URL with Nexscope

**Goal:** Paste an existing product listing, check the imported facts and images, prepare a script, choose video settings, and review the finished video before publishing it. This is Nexscope's **URL to Video** workflow, distinct from the image-to-video generator that starts with an uploaded photo.

## 1. Import a product page

Open [Nexscope URL to Video](https://www.nexscope.ai/url-to-video) and select **Create product video** to enter the workspace. Sign in when requested. Paste a link to an individual product page in **Product URL**, then select **Analyze URL**. The supported-platform list currently shows **Amazon, Shopify, Walmart, Etsy, and eBay**. A valid link is required; an unsupported, private, or inaccessible page may not import usable details.

Wait for **Product details ready**. Review the imported **Product name** and **Product description** against your live listing and edit anything inaccurate. In **Select product images**, choose the images that show the actual item clearly. You can change the selection and, where offered, add your own images. The page displays how many images can be selected for this workflow.

Importing saves copying time; it does not verify that a listing claim is true. Remove outdated prices, product promises, or promotion details before they reach a script.

## 2. Choose what the video should say

In **Script**, choose **Generate with AI** or **Write my own**. For the AI route, first choose a video angle and select **Generate AI script**. Read the proposed script, then edit or regenerate it if the hook, product facts, or offer is wrong. For the manual route, type your own text in **Video script**.

Keep the script specific to the product. For example, describe a real feature shown by the images and one buyer use case. Avoid adding a discount, capacity, safety, or performance claim that your listing and product evidence cannot support. If you add a promotion, check its conditions and end date in the final wording.

An avatar is optional. Choose a presenter only if it helps explain the product; a product-only video can be clearer for some listings. Review the selected image and script together before spending credits on generation.

## 3. Set the format and check the estimate

Under **Video settings**, choose the available **Model**, **Aspect ratio**, **Resolution**, **Duration**, **Target market**, and **Language**. Match the aspect ratio to the destination rather than choosing one by habit: for example, the workspace labels a 9:16 option for TikTok and a 16:9 option for Amazon. Check that your selected model supports the settings displayed.

Read the **estimated credit range** next to **Generate**. The final charge is based on the completed generation, so the estimate is a planning range, not a fixed bill. When the product details and script are ready, select **Generate** once and follow the task status instead of resubmitting while it works.

## 4. Inspect and download the result

The video appears in **Recent videos** when the task is ready; **View all history** opens the broader history. Preview the clip and check several frames: Is the item still the same shape and color? Are labels and packaging readable and accurate? Does the voice or caption make only claims you can support? Check the first seconds at the size and crop of your intended channel.

Use **Download video** only after the clip passes review. If it fails, use **Edit settings** to adjust the input before another generation. Keep a record of the source URL, approved product facts, selected images, script, model, settings, and date. A generated video is a creative asset—not evidence of product performance or an automatic sales lift.

**Try it with a product you know well:** [Open Nexscope URL to Video](https://www.nexscope.ai/url-to-video). If the import fails, share the platform, non-sensitive URL pattern, and error message in the [official Nexscope community](https://github.com/nexscope-ai/ecommerce-ai-tools/discussions).
