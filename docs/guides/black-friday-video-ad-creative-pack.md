# Black Friday video ads: turn one product photo into a creative test pack

**Build a small Black Friday creative pack around one exact product before generating for a whole catalog.** This exercise plans two image concepts, one motion clip and one presenter-led introduction. Keep the product facts fixed while testing the creative idea, then review every candidate before deciding what to publish.

## What should you prepare first?

Download the [one-product creative brief]({{ '/assets/downloads/black-friday-creative-brief.md' | relative_url }}) and [asset budget and review sheet]({{ '/assets/downloads/black-friday-creative-pack.csv' | relative_url }}). The brief contains an explicitly fictional example; the sheet is blank. Neither represents generated assets or a measured campaign.

Prepare one clear photo, the exact variant, approved product facts and your intended placement. Keep an additional detail photo ready if a model needs information the first image does not show. Having one reference is a starting point, not permission for a model to invent the hidden side of an item.

For TikTok Shop planning, TikTok's [October 14 announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/whats-shaping-the-holiday-season-on-tiktok-shop?lang=en) gives a November 11–30, 2026 campaign window. Confirm your own account's eligibility and deadlines separately. This guide covers creative production; use the [BFCM seller checklist]({{ '/ecommerce-trends/tiktok-shop-holiday-shopping-2026-seller-guide/' | relative_url }}) for broader readiness.

## Define a four-asset pack for one SKU

**Give each asset a job and an approval rule.** The suggested scope below is small enough to review manually:

| Asset | Creative job | Starting input | Approval rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clean still | Make the exact item clear | Approved product photo | Shape, label, color and quantity match |
| Gift-context still | Explore a relevant seasonal setting | Same item and factual scene brief | Scene adds no misleading accessories or offer |
| Motion clip | Show a simple camera or scene change | Approved photo and one motion direction | Item remains accurate throughout the clip |
| Presenter concept | Explain one verified feature | Approved image and short factual script | Presenter, product and speech remain consistent |

For a fictional desk organizer, the buyer question might be “Where can I keep the small things on my desk?” Use only visible, approved compartments in the scene. Do not add dimensions, a discount, a delivery guarantee or a claim of tested durability unless your records support it.

## Step 1: plan the two still images

Use the [product photography workflow]({{ '/ai-product-image-generator/' | relative_url }}) and its prompt pack. Start with a clean presentation, then change only the setting for the gift-context candidate. Keep offer copy separate from the product image until the offer is approved.

A suggested input is:

> Place the supplied desk organizer on a neutral desk with soft lighting. Preserve its compartments, color, shape and visible quantity. Add no accessories, text, discount or product claim. Leave room beside it for approved copy added later.

This is an unexecuted brief. If the output adds an extra compartment or changes the item, record a rejection. An attractive gift setting does not compensate for a different product.

## Step 2: create one product-motion candidate

Open [Image to Video](https://www.nexscope.ai/seller/video-generation/image-to-video?co-from=learn) with an approved image. Choose a simple movement, such as a slow camera push toward a stationary product. Select a supported duration and frame for your intended placement and check the estimate before submitting once.

For a first exercise, a short vertical clip can help evaluate a social placement, but the available settings depend on the selected model. Preserve the task record. If generation is slow, inspect the same task instead of submitting an identical paid job.

Review the entire result, not only its first frame. Pause on labels, corners, handles and contact points. Reject added items, changing proportions or motion that implies a product function you have not demonstrated. The [image-to-video guide]({{ '/ai-video-generator/' | relative_url }}) provides a broader review checklist.

## Step 3: prepare the presenter-led introduction

Use [Avatar Video](https://www.nexscope.ai/seller/video-generation/product-talking-video?co-from=learn) when a presenter helps explain a feature. Choose an available presenter and keep the script tied to the approved item:

| Part | Fictional organizer wording | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | “Need a place for the small things on your desk?” | Appropriate buyer use case |
| Feature | “These compartments keep everyday desk items separated.” | Matches the selected variant |
| Detail | “Check the size and included pieces on the product page.” | Correct current listing |
| Next step | “See whether it fits your setup.” | Correct item link |

Do not make the presenter claim a personal purchase or product test that never happened. Follow the [TikTok AI UGC script workflow]({{ '/ecommerce-trends/tiktok-shop-ai-ugc-video-seller-workflow/' | relative_url }}) for a playable existing example and detailed script review. That official example illustrates a format; it is not a result from this organizer brief.

## Step 4: approve the offer separately from the creative

**Product truth, promotion approval and media quality are different checks.** Confirm the exact variant, permitted discount, dates, inventory, delivery terms and destination requirements in the systems that own those facts. Use approved text in your editing or publishing process; this guide does not imply a particular in-app overlay editor.

Give each candidate an ID in the worksheet. Record model, settings, task, estimate, actual charge, refund if confirmed, reviewer and decision. A completed task can still be rejected for factual or visual reasons. Do not treat a creative rejection as an automatic refund.

Budget the initial jobs plus an explicit review reserve. Calculate net spend per approved asset by asset type, rather than counting a still image and a presenter video as interchangeable outputs. Use the [video cost worksheet]({{ '/guides/kling-ai-pricing/' | relative_url }}) for the related arithmetic.

## A suggested preparation sequence

For a team starting on November 6, the following is an **editorial schedule**, not an official submission deadline:

| Planning stage | Finish before moving on |
| --- | --- |
| Nov 6–7: product and brief | Approved SKU, permitted images, buyer question and known offer constraints |
| Nov 8: small creative pilot | Saved still and video candidates with their actual charges |
| Nov 9: review | Correct product, speech, text, crop and destination checks |
| Nov 10: readiness decision | Approved assets and verified account/offer settings, or a documented reason to hold |

Adapt this to your actual deadlines and team capacity. If an asset fails, hold it and simplify the brief. Once one product passes a repeatable review, the [Bulk Video guide]({{ '/guides/bulk-product-video-guide/' | relative_url }}) can help plan a later batch.

## What should you measure after publishing?

Keep the asset ID with placement, offer, market and publication date. Separate impressions and video viewing from product clicks, orders, refunds and returns. A changed product, discount or audience creates another test condition; it does not isolate the effect of a new hook.

Use the result to decide which product question deserves another creative treatment. This workflow does not promise distribution, advertising approval or a sales lift.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should a small Black Friday creative pack contain?

For this exercise: two still concepts, one motion clip and one presenter concept around one SKU. Change the scope when your placement or budget requires it.

### Does Nexscope automatically publish these ads?

The linked workflows prepare candidate media. Publish through the appropriate account only after the required product, offer and asset checks.

### Should I generate a whole catalog at once?

Start with one reviewed product. A batch makes sense after the input and approval process work reliably enough for your team.
