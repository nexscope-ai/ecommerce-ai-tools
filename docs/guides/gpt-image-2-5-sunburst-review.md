# GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst Review: Detail & User Feedback

GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst is one of two separately labelled 2.5 image routes on Nexscope. It should not be reviewed as though Sunburst and Flare were interchangeable. This article focuses on the evidence for composition stability and detail, with clear limits on what public comparisons can establish.

> **Our take:** Sunburst is worth comparing for detailed compositions and repeat editing, but public results do not establish that it is better for every image. ## What are its strengths?

The route provides several quality levels as well as resolution control. That makes it possible to evaluate a detailed commercial brief without assuming one fixed output setting suits every job. Reference images can be supplied for editing or subject guidance, while a text-only request can explore a new concept.

The strongest source-based reason to consider Sunburst is stability when refining an approved direction. A product shot that keeps its viewpoint and composition through revisions can reduce design disruption. However, this is a hypothesis supported by particular public examples, not an established acceptance advantage on the Nexscope route.

## What are its limitations?

Extra detail is not always extra accuracy. A model can add plausible objects or elaborate a scene beyond the requested brief. In product imagery, that can introduce accessories, materials or use conditions that should not appear. Evaluate additions as potential errors, not automatically as creative improvements.

No new Sunburst generation was run for this article. We have not measured its speed against Flare on Nexscope, nor verified equal credit charges across all settings. A third-party run time or another platform's price cannot be applied to this Playground.

## What do users say about GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst?

In a [same-prompt public comparison](https://www.reddit.com/r/ChatGPT/comments/1wihnzf/gpt_image_25_flare_vs_sunburst_on_the_same/), u/Practical_Low29 reported that Sunburst kept a product composition more stable across quality settings than Flare. The same author could not clearly separate them on one multi-edit chain, which limits the stronger claim that Sunburst always edits better. The post links provider-associated materials.

A [September 9, 2026 creator comparison](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SYShsjItheo) praised Sunburst's object and landscape detail but preferred the older Image 2 on some portrait examples. These are selected tasks, not an overall ranking or representative user survey. **Confidence: limited; useful comparisons with host and sample bias.**

## Who should consider it?

Consider Sunburst when preserving an approved composition and rendering detail matter enough to justify evaluating it separately. Do not assume the largest quality setting is always the best commercial choice. Compare the actual subject, scene and revision behaviour with Flare before deciding which belongs in your workflow.
