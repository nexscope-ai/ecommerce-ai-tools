# How to prepare TikTok Shop auto-post carousels for one product

Start with one product ID and one offer. Give each image a different job, then check the images against the exact variant shoppers can buy. TikTok Shop's auto-post carousel feature can assemble product photos for distribution in the For You feed. Access and available controls depend on the seller account. [TikTok Shop Auto-post Carousels guide](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6371898581600013&lang=en)

## Build a photo set around one offer

For an example, use a navy travel mug sold as one mug with one black lid. Brief four images and match them to the same public product listing.

| Image | What to show | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead photo | The navy mug clearly visible in a vertical composition | The shape, finish and lid match the linked product |
| Use scene | The mug in a measured cup holder | Any fit claim refers to verified dimensions |
| Feature | A close-up of the lid or rim | The image shows the part the buyer receives |
| Package contents | One mug and one lid together | Quantity and included parts match the checkout offer |

Keep essential product details legible even when a photo is viewed on its own. If a holiday price or claim is baked into an image, note the date it expires and check whether the account offers an asset end date.

## Review the assets in Seller Center

1. Open **Content Hub → Carousel** or **Shoppable Carousels**, depending on the view available in your account. Find the product by ID.
2. If your account offers **Add creatives** under auto-post photos, choose the product, upload the approved photo set and check its review status. If the control is absent, check your account's available carousel options.
3. Use the library to inspect photos for that product. Open the full preview if available. Check the color, quantity and offer against the linked product page. Review expiring photos before the campaign changes. [TikTok asset upload instructions](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?default_language=en&knowledge_id=6642198357804814)
