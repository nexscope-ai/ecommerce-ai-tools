# Product description rewrite: turn verified facts into clear product copy

**A product description rewrite should make the offer easier to understand while preserving the facts.** Start with what is included, who the item may suit and what the buyer still needs to know. Nexscope's product copy rewriting service turns observed page issues into suggested titles, selling points, descriptions and FAQs for your review.

## Decide which part of the copy is failing

A crowded title and an incomplete description need different edits. An ambiguous specification needs confirmation before either can be rewritten. Give the report a clear objective, such as explaining the included kit, distinguishing variants or answering a recurring setup question.

Use the [copy fact-check worksheet]({{ '/assets/downloads/product-copy-fact-check.csv' | relative_url }}) to retain the source, proposed wording and approval for each claim. Leave an unsupported claim unresolved rather than filling it with a plausible benefit.

| Copy element | Main job | Typical question to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | Identify the product and important variant | Can the reader recognise the exact offer? |
| Selling points | Explain useful choices and included features | What does this fact mean for the buyer's task? |
| Description | Connect the facts into a readable explanation | How should the buyer understand the setup and use? |
| FAQ | Resolve a specific uncertainty | What remains unclear after the main information? |

Shopify's [product description guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/details/product-descriptions) likewise frames descriptions around useful features and benefits. Your chosen marketplace or store still governs the field format and publishing process.

## Build a fact sheet before a persuasive paragraph

Include the exact product and variant, contents, measurements with endpoints, supported uses, exclusions and approved brand language. Separate a measured fact from a seller assertion and an unresolved question.

For example, a working-length statement may depend on which components are attached. A writer cannot settle that by replacing it with a cleaner but different number. Ask the owner to confirm the configuration first.

Also specify the market and report language. A British-English draft for a UK listing is a defined request, not a promise that the same terminology or field limits fit every marketplace.

## Read a real draft in context

The [copywriting section of Nexscope's public report](https://www.nexscope.ai/reports/product-growth-example-en.html?co-from=learn#module-copywriting) provides a British-English title, five selling points and a description for the PCIQUTE kit. The November 5, 2026 example retains the brand, kit contents, roller sizes and approximate working-length basis.

The important pattern is that the proposed text follows the underlying findings. It does not add a larger reach claim simply because an adjacent search phrase exists. A rewrite can make information clearer without declaring the product stronger, safer or better than an alternative.

For your own copy review, use this sequence:

1. Match each numerical statement to a current source or measurement.
2. Check that the included and excluded items match the sold variant.
3. Distinguish a useful feature from an unsupported performance outcome.
4. Read the title and first selling point together for duplication or missing identity.
5. Check the final fields and mobile layout before publication.

The example is a Nexscope-published reference, not evidence of a conversion improvement after the draft was applied.

## Keep page copy and ad copy separate

A complete product description and a short campaign headline solve different jobs. If you request [advertising images]({{ '/guides/product-ad-image-brief/' | relative_url }}), a brief caption or associated image language does not imply that you also purchased a full listing rewrite.

Confirm each destination's needs. A concise headline may be useful beside a product image, while the page still needs details about contents and assembly. Do not allow a campaign concept to introduce claims absent from the approved page.

## Check the result after publication

Keep the previous copy and a dated change record. Ask whether the revised page answers the intended buyer question before looking for performance changes. Use your own analytics or a suitable experiment to investigate outcomes; do not treat a rewritten paragraph as proof that orders will rise.

## Frequently asked questions

### What can the product copy rewriting service provide?

It can provide suggested titles, selling points, descriptions and FAQs grounded in available product facts. You review and apply the draft through your own listing or store workflow.

### Can a rewrite add claims that competitors use?

Not without supporting evidence for your own product. Another listing or a generated suggestion does not establish your specifications, certification or performance.

### Is advertising-image text the same as a complete listing rewrite?

No. Brief image text or accompanying advertising language does not mean a full product-page rewrite is included. Select product copy rewriting when that is the deliverable you need.
