# How to set up a ChatGPT Business Agent for your online store

Start with one product and four questions a shopper needs answered before checkout. Use your approved product page and store policies as the source for those answers. OpenAI introduced Business Agents with Businesses in ChatGPT on November 10, 2026. Agents are initially available to eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses with a website. [OpenAI launch announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) · [Eligibility and setup](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt)

{% include article-quick-action.html %}

## Open the agent preview after verification

1. Claim your business in Business Hub and complete Persona identity verification. If you already completed the Shopify setup, check for an existing agent draft.
2. Open your verified business in the Business tab. Open the draft or create an agent if your account offers the option.
3. Review the product facts and policy information the agent will use. Preview the agent with the questions below. Correct any unsupported answers before publishing. [OpenAI setup instructions](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt#set-up-and-publish-your-agent)

Customers who choose to start a Business Agent conversation enter a labeled chat with that business. Keep agent preview results separate from observations about how independent ChatGPT answers describe your product. [OpenAI Business Agents policy](https://openai.com/policies/business-agents-policy/)

## Copy this one-SKU Q&A template

Choose an exact product variant and write the expected answer before opening the preview. Replace the example with verified facts and links from your own store.

| Ask the agent | Expected answer to prepare | Source to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What comes with the navy mug? | Exact pack quantity, lid type and included accessories | Live variant page or approved pack specification |
| Will this fit my 2.8-inch cup holder? | Compare the verified base diameter with the opening and mention any missing fit details | Measured base dimensions for that exact SKU |
| Can it arrive by Friday in my ZIP code? | Direct the customer to the current delivery estimate when an exact date cannot be verified | Shipping policy and checkout estimate |
| Can I return a mug bought on sale? | Explain the documented sale-item conditions and return window | Current return policy |

Use a simple record for each preview. Save the exact question, expected answer, source URL, preview answer, discrepancy and correction. Try one follow-up that changes the size or destination to check whether the agent carries the right facts across turns.

## Fix the source when the answer exposes a gap

If the agent guesses a dimension or combines the contents of two variants, first confirm the underlying fact. Then revise the relevant product field, policy or agent guidance and rerun the same preview question. When the exact size or delivery estimate is unavailable, guide the shopper to the product page or checkout for the current information.
