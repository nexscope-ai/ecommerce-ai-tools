# Why isn't my Shopify product showing up in ChatGPT?

Start with one product and its exact variant. Check whether Shopify can share it through Catalog, then inspect what ChatGPT returns for a buying question in that market. An eligible item can still be absent from a particular answer. [Shopify’s guide to selling on ChatGPT](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/chatgpt)

{% include article-quick-action.html %}

## 1. Check the channel and the product in Shopify

In Shopify admin, open **Sales channels → Agentic → ChatGPT** and inspect Catalog access. Shopify says its ChatGPT channel is active by default for eligible stores. Its documented requirements include selling to US customers, Shopify Catalog eligibility, accepting the applicable terms and completing the required store policies. A store can be based outside the US if it sells to US customers and meets the other requirements. [Shopify channel requirements](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/chatgpt#requirements-for-selling-on-chatgpt)

| Check | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Market | Whether the store sells to the US and whether the item is available for that market |
| Catalog | The channel access setting and any eligibility status Shopify shows for the product |
| Product identity | The live URL, title, brand, model and exact variant |
| Offer | The selected variant's current price, currency and availability |
| Landing page | What a US shopper can verify about that variant, including what is included and applicable return terms |

Open the live page as a shopper would. Select the same variant and compare the displayed offer with your Shopify records. For example, if the default image shows a two-pack but the linked variant is a single item, clarify the title, image and pack quantity before retesting.

## 2. Check the answer for the exact item

Run one category question and one question that includes your brand or model. For a hypothetical insulated travel mug, ask “Which insulated mugs fit a narrow car cup holder and have a lid I can take apart to clean?” Then ask about the exact model and compare its stated base diameter and lid design with the live page.

Save the question, date, target market, answer and linked product URL if one appears. In your notes, record each outcome separately:

- The answer names the brand.
- The answer identifies the exact product and variant.
- The answer explicitly recommends it for the stated use.
- The linked offer shows matching details.

If the answer links to a different size or pack, flag the mismatch. Confirm which facts are on the product page and which still need a verified measurement or clearer copy. One answer is a dated observation. Repeat the same question with comparable settings after making an update.

## 3. Decide whether to fix Catalog access or product information

If Catalog access or eligibility is unresolved, start in Shopify admin. If the item is eligible and the answer still identifies the wrong model, review the product identity, variant names, attributes and supporting facts.
