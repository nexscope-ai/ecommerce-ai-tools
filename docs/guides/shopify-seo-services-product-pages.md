# Shopify SEO Services: When to Start With One Product Page

A Shopify SEO service should match the level of the problem. A store-wide pattern across collections, locales or product templates calls for broader technical work. A healthy store with one strategically important product can start with a single product-page review and a short list of changes your team can apply.

## Compare three Shopify service scopes

| Service scope | Bring to the review | Ask for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store technical audit | Affected collections, a few product URLs and examples of search issues | A diagnosis by template or URL pattern, with validation steps |
| Product page review | One public Shopify product URL, its target market and variant | Specific findings about buyer intent, product facts, copy or comparison |
| Ongoing implementation | A prioritized backlog, the current theme and owners | Who edits the theme, who approves copy and how changes will be tested |

If a theme change is needed, ask the provider to show which product templates it affects. If the work is editorial, ask for an example title and a list of facts the merchant should verify before publishing. Those requests make it easier to compare two quotes that both say “Shopify SEO.”

## Give the reviewer the same page a buyer sees

Open the product URL with no admin session and select the market and variant you want reviewed. Record the product title, actual materials or dimensions, price and included items. If your partner reviews the US page while you sell the reviewed SKU in Canada, the store can show a different offer or availability. State the market in the request.

Shopify lets you edit a product’s search listing in the product editor. Record the copy and URL before editing so you can tell what changed. [Shopify product editing guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/add-update-products)

## Start with a decision your team can act on

For example, a seller with two nearly identical jackets could ask whether the winter version clearly explains its insulation and use case. The desired output is a list of facts and copy changes for that specific page. If every jacket’s variants open the wrong option, start with the [Shopify product SEO audit]({{ '/guides/shopify-product-page-seo-audit/' | relative_url }}) and check for a theme or feed issue first.

If you have one clear product-level question, send the URL and market to Nexscope and select the report service that answers it. Keep the before-and-after copy or screenshots in your own records so you can verify what shipped.
