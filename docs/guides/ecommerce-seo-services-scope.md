# Ecommerce SEO Services: Choose the Right Scope for Your Store

Choose ecommerce SEO services based on where the problem appears. If several collections disappear from Google or thousands of URLs are duplicated, start with a store-wide technical audit. If a single product underperforms while similar pages still attract shoppers, start with that product URL and the specific decision you need to make.

## Match the service to the work

| What you see | What to ask for | What to review before paying |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Several categories lost visibility at once | Technical audit across representative templates | Affected URL patterns, test URLs, and the owner of each fix |
| One product receives impressions but few clicks | Product page and search-intent review | Relevant queries, competing pages, and a proposed title or content change |
| Shoppers visit but still ask basic questions | Product content review | Missing dimensions, compatibility, included items, or other purchase details |
| A backlog already exists and nobody can implement it | Ongoing implementation | Who makes changes, priorities, reporting, and ownership of access |

Ask a provider to show one finding from a sample deliverable. “Improve your product content” gives your team little to act on. “The page shows a two-pack in the hero image, but the selected option ships one unit. Update the variant image and the pack-size text beside Add to cart” gives the designer and merchandiser something they can verify.

## Build a request around one decision

Pick a product with a clear business reason to act. It could be a high-margin item with steady impressions or a seasonal product that needs a clearer comparison before a campaign. Record its public URL, target market and one question, such as “Does the page explain why buyers should choose the 2 L model over the 1 L model?” Include the dates of any recent price, stock or content changes so the reviewer can distinguish changes that happened together.

For one product, request the type of help you actually need. Nexscope’s report offers options such as competitor analysis, keyword research, copy and ad images. Your team applies the output. For a catalog-wide issue, start with the [ecommerce SEO audit checklist]({{ '/ecommerce-seo-audit/' | relative_url }}).

## Compare quotes by deliverable

Ask how many URLs or products the quote covers, whether implementation is included, and what evidence will support the recommendations. Agree on the first outcome you can verify, such as a fixed indexability issue, a corrected product fact, or a revised description ready for review. Record traffic and order metrics separately from those delivery milestones.
