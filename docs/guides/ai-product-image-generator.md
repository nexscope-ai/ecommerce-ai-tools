# AI product photography: generate and check product images

**By Nexscope Team · Published September 23, 2026 · Reviewed October 23, 2026**

**AI product photography** uses an image-generation model to explore product shots, backgrounds and lifestyle concepts. An AI product image generator can work from a scene description or a supported product reference, but an attractive image is not automatically an accurate listing image. Choose a model, inspect the cost and compare the result with the real item before publishing.

**[Open the AI Product Image Generator](https://www.nexscope.ai/tools/ai-image-generator?co-from=learn).** New Nexscope users receive 1,000 free credits, valid for 3 days; usage varies by model and settings. Creative API key access requires an active subscription.

> **Quick answer:** Start with one clear product reference when the chosen model supports it. Describe the scene without inventing product features. Generate at an aspect ratio suited to the destination, and check the shape, packaging, label, color, and claims before downloading.

## When is an AI product image useful?

| Task | Good starting material | What a human must verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explore a lifestyle scene | A real product photo and a short scene brief | The product still looks like the item for sale. |
| Draft an ad concept | Brand-approved product facts and campaign requirements | Text, claims, and visual implications are supported. |
| Compare creative directions | The same reference and prompt across available models | Differences in fidelity, framing, and actual credit use. |

The tool creates candidate media. It does not verify that the output complies with a marketplace's current rules or that a depicted feature exists.

## How do you generate and check an image?

1. Gather a high-quality photo of the real product, its approved specifications, and the destination image requirements. Decide whether you need a clean product view, a lifestyle scene, or an ad concept.
2. Open the [image generator](https://www.nexscope.ai/tools/ai-image-generator?co-from=learn) and sign in when prompted.
3. Select an available model. The live selector shows which inputs and output settings that model supports. Use a reference image if the selected model permits one; otherwise use an accurate text description.
4. Write one clear prompt describing the subject, background, light, framing, and details that must stay unchanged. Avoid asking the model to add unverified features.
5. Choose the available aspect ratio, resolution, output count, and other settings. Review the displayed credit estimate before submitting.
6. Generate, preview, and compare the output with the actual product and your approved copy. Download only an image that passes your review.
7. Save the reference, prompt, model, settings, date, output, and actual credits used. Change one variable at a time if you run another comparison.

The model catalog and settings can change. The live tool is the source for current availability and estimates; this guide does not rank the models or promise a particular output.

## What should a product-image prompt say?

For a *hypothetical* stainless-steel water bottle, a starting prompt might be:

> Create a clean studio image of the supplied bottle on a neutral surface. Keep the bottle silhouette, cap, color, and printed label consistent with the reference. Use soft side lighting and leave space around the product. Do not add accessories, capacity text, or features absent from the reference.

This is an example input, **not a measured result**. A prompt can request fidelity but cannot guarantee it. If the output changes the logo or cap, reject it or revise the input and review a new generation.

## What evidence should you inspect before publishing?

| Check | Compare with | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Real product photo and packaging artwork | Reject wrong colors, proportions, logos, or labels. |
| Product claims | Approved specifications and test evidence | Remove imagery that suggests unsupported performance or included accessories. |
| Composition | Destination placement and current platform rules | Crop or regenerate if the product is obscured or the format is unsuitable. |
| Economics | On-page estimate and completed run's credit usage | Keep the settings and cost with the selected asset. |
